# T68 java.sql.Timestamp

Implemented: `java.sql.Timestamp extends java.util.Date` in the bundled runtime — `Timestamp(long)`,
`setTime`, `getTime`, `getNanos`, `setNanos`, `equals(Timestamp)`, `equals(Object)`, `before(Timestamp)`,
`after(Timestamp)`, `compareTo(Timestamp)`, `compareTo(Date)`, `hashCode`, `from(Instant)`, `toInstant()`.

Left out: `toString()`/`valueOf(String)` (default-time-zone formatting through deprecated `Date` fields,
non-deterministic across hosts), `valueOf(LocalDateTime)` and `toLocalDateTime()` (no `java.time.LocalDateTime
in the runtime), the deprecated `Timestamp(int,...,int)` calendar constructor and inherited deprecated
accessors, `serialVersionUID`/`writeObject`/`readObject`.

Notes: `BytecodeTimestampTest` passed the first `kit check` run, matching the task's calibration. The
overflow fixtures (`Long.MIN_VALUE` constructor/`setTime`, `Timestamp.from` on `Long.MAX_VALUE/1000+1`
seconds) wrap silently in Wasm exactly as on the JVM because every formula is plain `/`, `%` and `*` with
no `Math.*Exact`.
