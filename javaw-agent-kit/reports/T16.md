# T16 Checksum, CRC32 and Adler32

Implemented: `java.util.zip.Checksum` (interface with abstract `update(int)`, `update(byte[],int,int)`, `getValue()`,
`reset()` and the default `update(byte[])` delegating to `update(b, 0, b.length)`), `java.util.zip.CRC32` (reflected
CRC-32, polynomial `0xEDB88320`, 256-entry static table, `crc` holds the finished value, 0 at start,
`getValue() = (long) crc & 0xffffffffL`, `reset()` to 0) and `java.util.zip.Adler32` (adler starts at 1, `% 65521`
after every byte, `reset()` to 1). Both `update(byte[],int,int)` check `NullPointerException` (no message) before the
JDK `Range [off, off + len) out of bounds for length n` `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` check; the shared
`checkRange` is a package-private static on `CRC32`, reused by `Adler32` as the task calibration suggests. Registered
all three with `kit register`. `BytecodeChecksumTest` passed on the first `kit check` (1 run).

Left out: `Checksum.update(ByteBuffer)`, `CRC32.update(ByteBuffer)`, `Adler32.update(ByteBuffer)` (no
`java.nio.ByteBuffer` in the runtime; the test checks they stay unlinked) and `java.util.zip.CRC32C` (different
polynomial, not needed yet). No `synchronized`, no native, no `java.nio` imports.

Notes: pure arithmetic, no surprises — matched the JVM vectors (CRC `cbf43926` for "123456789", unsigned
`3421780262`, Adler `11e60398` for "Wikipedia") on the first run, including the 120000-byte 0xff overflow cases and
the literal `off + " + " + len` range-message text.
