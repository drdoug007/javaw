`remove(Object,Object)`, `replace(K,V,V)`, `replace(K,V)`; defaults `getOrDefault`, `forEach`, `replaceAll`,
`computeIfAbsent`, `computeIfPresent`, `compute`, `merge`, copied from the JDK 21 source with identical call

`kit register java/util/concurrent/ConcurrentMap.java` (`paths.add` line).

test asserts they stay unlinked.
